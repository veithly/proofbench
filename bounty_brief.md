# Bounty Brief: Mantle Turing Test Hackathon 2026

## Hackathon
- Name: The Turing Test Hackathon 2026, Phase 2: AI Awakening.
- URL: https://dorahacks.io/hackathon/mantleturingtesthackathon2026/detail

## Theme
Mantle is inviting builders to deploy autonomous agents on Mantle and make agent decisions/outcomes visible as on-chain benchmark evidence.

## Tracks
- AI Trading & Strategy
- AI Alpha & Data
- AI x RWA
- Consumer & Viral DApps
- AI DevTools
- Agentic Wallets & Economy

## Prize Signals
- Phase 2 total prize pool: $100K.
- Grand Champion: business potential, completion, Mantle ecosystem fit.
- Track First Prize: one winner per track.
- Best UI/UX Award: best user experience and smoothest onboarding for Web2 users.
- Deployment Award: top finalists deployed on Mantle.

## Sponsor / Domain Primitives
- Mantle public chain data and explorer evidence.
- Agent decision and outcome records.
- ERC-8004-style agent identity and reputation.
- Optional Mantle Sepolia contract deployment.
- Bybit/Byreal ecosystem relevance where appropriate.

## Hard Constraints
- No generic chatbot, wallet dashboard, upload-summary app, or static analytics dashboard.
- First meaningful action must work without a wallet wall.
- The first 60 seconds must produce a visible state change and durable proof artifact.
- Do not fake on-chain writes. If no funded key/wallet exists, mark notarization as optional and show calldata honestly.

## Current Leading Concept From First Pass
ProofSpring: Verify 1 Mantle transaction with an agent decision receipt in 60 seconds.
