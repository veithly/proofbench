# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Mantle agent builders, task sponsors, protocol operators, and hackathon judges who need to inspect whether an autonomous agent completed one bounded paid task. They review under time pressure and need the evaluator, payout state, reputation delta, and Mantle proof in one place.

## Product Purpose
ProofBench scores 1 agent task into a Mantle payout receipt in 60 seconds. Success means a fresh visitor can run a wallet-free quote-guard task, see a deterministic PASS/FAIL, watch payout/no-payout and reputation update, and open a receipt with proof hash and Mantle Sepolia calldata/event status.

## Brand Personality
Precise, accountable, quietly sharp. ProofBench should feel like an evidence desk for agent work, not a crypto casino, marketplace directory, or generic AI dashboard.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic dark DeFi dashboards, purple AI gradient heroes, explorer clones, wallet portfolio pages, agent marketplace cards, fake activity feeds, and text-only audit chatbots.

## Design Principles
- Show proof before persuasion.
- Make the first action wallet-free.
- Treat every agent payout as a record.
- Keep technical density readable under judge time pressure.
- Mark limitations honestly, especially optional chain writes and simulated payout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast or better. Verdicts and payout states must not rely on color alone. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. Mobile receipt review must keep hashes readable without horizontal page overflow.
